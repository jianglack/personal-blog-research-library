Test-Time Compute for Large Language Models

2026-05-28 · 38 min · en · LLM / Reasoning

LLMReasoningSearch

A structured note on methods, benefits, and limits of test-time compute. This layout keeps the article unframed, readable, and close to AstroPaper.

Summary

Test-time compute describes techniques that spend more inference-time work to improve model outputs. The main categories are sampling, search, and verification.

Methods

Sampling more candidates, searching over reasoning paths, and verifying candidate answers each trade latency for reliability in different ways.

for candidate in samples:
    score = verifier(candidate)
    keep_best(candidate, score)

Open Questions

When does extra compute stop helping? How should reasoning quality be measured? What is the right tradeoff between latency and reliability?
